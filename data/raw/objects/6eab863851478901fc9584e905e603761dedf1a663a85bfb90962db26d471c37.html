 2 Per Player 192 Dominate every lane in this Team Trios Sealed event! This event is excellent for casual Riftbound players.

Event cap is 192 player/64 teams. Players enroll individually, then can group as teams. Within each team of three, players will assign themselves A, B, or C. Players may not change their designation throughout the event. Player “B” will be the team captain, and will receive round pairings for the team.
At the start of the event, teams will be given 15 Vendetta booster packs and 90 minutes to open their packs and build three decks, one for each player, following Sealed Deck construction rules.
Each round, they’ll play a Best of 1, 1v1 match against the player with the same designation on the opposing team. The team that wins 2 matches (of 3) wins the round.
5 Rounds.
Rounds have a 30 minute time limit.
Prizes are awarded by the end of Round 5.
