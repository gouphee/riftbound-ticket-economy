## Payout-function review

- `src/riftbound_tickets/payouts/rules.py` distinguishes unknown from zero, enforces valid records, and does not round draws into wins.
- Saturday tier boundaries sum to **4,992** tickets when all Top 128 placements are awarded. Top 8 sums to **1,728**; the 216-each equal split also sums to 1,728. The split changes individual allocation but does not generate extra tickets.
- A cut with missing Top 8 matches cannot support exact individual first/eighth finishes. The separate fixed rank-slot pool can still be calculated as a **conditional published-schedule pool**, not evidence of physical payout.
- Archived tests cover tier boundaries and no extra tickets for byes. The 29-test passing report came from Codex's handoff; tests were not re-executed for this audit.

## Highest-impact unresolved evidence

1. **Utrecht Sunday:** 268 records at prize levels are marked dropped, with no drop timing. The 10,920 published-rule subtotal versus 2,080 supported-eligibility subtotal illustrates why eligibility cannot be silently inferred.
2. **Incomplete Saturday brackets:** Vancouver has 8 unknown Top 8 individual payouts; Utrecht 7; Singapore 4; Los Angeles 8. Missing cut matches are explicitly retained as unresolved instead of treating Swiss seeds as final placements.
3. **Atlanta Friday:** 1,020 roster versus 1,019 standings rows; no fabricated result for the roster-only registration.
4. **Hartford Friday:** one record lacks corresponding match evidence and two prize-level drops have unknown eligibility.
5. **Singapore Friday:** event data exposes six completed rounds despite the published five-round table; published-rule subtotal is usable with its recorded conflict, not as an unqualified exact total.
6. **Other scattered drops:** Sydney, Vancouver, Barcelona, Singapore and Los Angeles include additional eligibility uncertainties; see the row-level issue ledger.

## Audit assessment

The methodological safeguards in the reviewed documentation, functions and validation code are sound starting points: provenance is explicit, Swiss seeds are not automatically considered Top 8 final results, and missing evidence stays unknown. The current evidence **does not support** claims of exact physical issuance, that every past Top 8 definitely split, or that unresolved dropped players received no prizes.

## Research-use clarification after participant feedback

The six-event strict gate is **not** a claim that only six events have usable standings-implied prize totals. For our economics analysis, qualifying recorded win results are included in the primary standings-implied calculation even when UVS status is `DROPPED`; uncertain eligibility remains a separate sensitivity. All eight Saturday observed fields exceed the Top 128 threshold, so the conditional advertised payout-pool benchmark is **8 × 4,992 = 39,936 tickets**, regardless of incomplete cut results. Friday and Sunday additionally need an **observed-results total versus feasible theoretical maximum** and measured match draw rates, as specified in `docs/MODELING_SPEC.md`. These are research metrics, not proof of physical issuance.
